## Decision

**No amendment to teaching, the skill, questions, scoring, model, or effort.** Continue with the frozen six confirmation runs for seeds 211 and 509. Skill SHA-256 remains `4741578910c18e412a9f4d6c5c8c019cfb2964a38803971cce11ad678b991260`; frozen data SHA-256 remains `6398bb1d71d2e2871790a756ac549c1f1cf391f8013fe2d1468e608df0e5d0d5`.

There is no demonstrated attributable teaching defect. A ceiling effect is a benchmark limitation, not a reason to add instructions or claim tutoring superiority. No corrective adaptive feedback branch was needed in development, so the experiment has not tested its benefit. The tutor's extra 20 prompt words per session did not improve an already-perfect score.

Confirmation can reproduce this result on the other frozen worlds; it cannot rescue the sensitivity problem or establish human learning. Any harder tasks, changed exposure, retained-memory checks, or additional runs belong to a separately authorized, newly frozen experiment. Stop this experiment after the twelve planned sessions.
