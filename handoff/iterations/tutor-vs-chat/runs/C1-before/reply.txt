Your two original pairs are correct **for the order A → B → D**, but your information-loss explanation is backwards.

Trace the slots:

\[
(u,v)\xrightarrow{A}(u+v,v)\xrightarrow{B}(v,u+v)\xrightarrow{D}(v,v)
\]

So \(D\) does overwrite the second slot—but at that moment the second slot contains \(u+v\), not the original \(v\). The value copied into both slots is the current first slot, which is \(v\), the **original second number**.

Therefore:

- final \((5,5)\) forces \(v=5\);
- the original first number \(u\) is lost;
- the original second number survives;
- all originals are \((u,5)\) for \(u=0,\dots,6\).

So \((0,5)\) and \((1,5)\) are valid, but they are not the complete set. If the question asked for all originals, there are seven.

Your reversal of \(A,B\) is right:

\[
B^{-1}(3,5)=(5,3),\qquad A^{-1}(5,3)=(5-3,3)=(2,3).
\]

Also note that order matters: \(A,D,B\) gives different preimages, so always record the order explicitly.
