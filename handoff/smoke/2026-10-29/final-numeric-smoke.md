## Tutor

Not quite right. Let me check it with you.

The gap from 0.9999999 up to 1 is exactly:

1 − 0.9999999 = 0.0000001

So 0.9999999 is **exactly 0.0000001 away** from 1 — not closer than that.

And if you meant the other comparison — "0.9999999 is closer to 1 than 0.0000001 is to 0" — those are a tie. The gap from 0.0000001 to 0 is also exactly 0.0000001, so both numbers sit the same distance from their targets.

A quick way to see it: count the nines. 0.9999999 has seven nines, so the distance to 1 is a 1 in the seventh decimal place — 0.0000001. A number like 0.99999999 (eight nines) would be ten times closer.
