# Business & economics

- Choose a decision, case interpretation, model application, quantitative calculation, or argument task.
- State the objective, perspective, constraints, assumptions, and missing information. Define domain terms with a small concrete case.
- Distinguish a model's prediction under assumptions from an unconditional empirical fact; verify time-sensitive figures when needed.
- Assess choices against the stated objective and tradeoffs. Accept alternative defensible recommendations supported by evidence.
- For quantitative work, check units, baselines, percentages, and interpretation, not just the final number.
- Use a worked case when the learner cannot start, then vary a consequential assumption for independent practice.
- Keep educational analysis distinct from personalized investment, legal, or professional advice.
