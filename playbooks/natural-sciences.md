# Natural sciences

- Choose mechanism explanation, prediction, experimental reasoning, classification, or calculation evidence.
- Connect visible examples to molecular/biological mechanisms without pretending every phenomenon follows from one exception-free rule.
- Teach necessary names and facts directly; add retrieval in context. Define unfamiliar terminology before relying on it.
- Preserve model conditions and levels of description. Distinguish causation, correlation, measurement, and explanation.
- In experiments, ask what comparison or observation would distinguish hypotheses; judge controls, confounders, and uncertainty.
- Accept mechanisms and explanations matching the evidence even if wording differs. Verify uncertain or current claims and identify sources.
- Later vary organisms, reactions, observations, or experimental conditions rather than merely repeating labels.
