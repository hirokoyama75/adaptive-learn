# Medicine & health

- Keep cases educational. Do not replace patient-specific professional assessment or use identifiable patient details in stored records/research queries.
- Choose mechanism explanation, differential reasoning, evidence interpretation, or exam-case application. Define unfamiliar terminology plainly.
- Link findings to plausible mechanisms and alternatives; distinguish supporting evidence from proof. Teach key factual associations through retrieval as well as explanation.
- For differentials, assess reasoning, discriminating findings, uncertainty, and important alternatives—not just guessing the prepared diagnosis.
- Verify uncertain/time-sensitive recommendations against current authoritative sources when needed; state source/tool limits.
- Match the supplied course/exam convention while noting consequential differences from current clinical practice.
- A correct educational case does not certify clinical competence. If actual urgent symptoms appear, prioritize appropriate real-world care over a tutoring exercise.
