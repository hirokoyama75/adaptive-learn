# Physics & engineering

- Choose prediction, model selection, calculation, experimental interpretation, or design as the actual task.
- State the system, assumptions, units, sign conventions, and neglected effects; define each unfamiliar name/symbol concretely.
- Check units, limiting cases, conservation where applicable, and plausibility. Correct arithmetic does not validate an inappropriate model.
- Use predict → explain/model → compare when productive, not as a compulsory question sequence.
- Assess engineering alternatives against supplied constraints, safety margins, and tradeoffs; several designs may be valid.
- Use math/proof guidance only for a concrete missing need. Later vary the physical situation or relax an assumption.
- Distinguish educational calculations from professional safety certification; do not imply a chat validated a real structure or device.
